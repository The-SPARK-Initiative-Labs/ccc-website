How a job works

From first message to a working PC.

  1. Call or text

    Tell me what's going on. Quick questions and advice are free.

  2. Diagnose

    $39 bench diagnostic. You get a plain-English answer and a price before any repair starts.

  3. Approve

    If parts are needed, you get options with total prices. Nothing is bought until you reply.

  4. Fix and test

    Repairs, then stress tests with logged temperatures, so it's proven working, not just booting.

  5. Report

    Photos, test results and everything that was done, in a report you keep.
